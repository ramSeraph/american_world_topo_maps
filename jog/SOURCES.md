# Joint Operations Graphic sources

This collection brings together scans from the following sources. The
[STAC collection](https://indianopenmaps.com/stac/collections/world-topo-american-jog)
identifies the selected source for each sheet and links to its original and
georeferenced files. The
[bounds file](https://github.com/ramSeraph/american_world_topo_maps/releases/download/jog-georef/bounds.geojson)
also records sheet-level provenance where available.

- **University of Texas Libraries** — most of the historical `jog_alt`
  collection came from the
  [Perry-Castañeda Library JOG collection](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/jog/).
  The files were restored from `to_share_temp/jog_alt`. Five sampled PDFs and
  JPEGs are byte-for-byte identical to their Texas catalogue downloads.
- **University of California, Berkeley** — [Earth Sciences & Map Library digital collections](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/).
- **University of Kansas** — [Joint Operations Graphics collection](https://libgis.ku.edu/jogs/).
- **National Library of Australia** — [JOG catalogue search](https://catalogue.nla.gov.au/Search/Home?lookfor=joint+operations+graphic).
- **ANU Asia-Pacific Map Collection** — [collection on JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/site/anu/antique-maps-kessler/).
- **Russian, Soviet, and East European Maps Foundation** — [JOG collection](https://www.rtrfoundation.org/jog.shtml).
- **Southeast Asia map scans** — [archived catalogue and sheet sections](https://web.archive.org/web/20120621011842/http://www.nexus.net/~911gfx/sea-ao.html).
- **US Geological Survey** — [archived GeoPDF collection](https://web.archive.org/web/*/https://deltas.usgs.gov/fm/data2/jog/GeoPDF_250K/*).
- **European Soil Data Centre** — [European Digital Archive of Soil Maps](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/european-digital-archive-soil-maps-eudasm).
- **Digital Commonwealth** — [digital collections](https://www.digitalcommonwealth.org/).
- **Mapstor** — scans from the
  [Internet Archive topographic maps collection](https://archive.org/details/topographic-maps).

## New Zealand

The New Zealand JOG (Air) sheets are from the National Library of New Zealand's
digital collection. The library's records and IIIF manifests permit the images
to be used, copied, and redistributed free of charge in any format or medium.
They request this acknowledgement when images are redistributed:

> Sourced from LINZ. Crown Copyright reserved.

For example, see the
[National Library record for East Cape, sheet 5](https://natlib.govt.nz/records/37100336).
This acknowledgement is also included in the published basemap attribution.

Copyright and reuse terms for other source material depend on the source and
individual item. Consult the linked source records for their terms.
